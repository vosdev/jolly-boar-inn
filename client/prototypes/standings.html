Standings: the Wise Old Man's hiscores

Each frame is a phone or a small window with the 56px top bar; scroll inside it. Between rounds the rows start in the last standings' order, the gains count up, and the rows move to their new places. The final plays fifth to first with a trophy each, then shows everyone. Holds start at 1.5×, the timing chosen for the app. Pick a scene and press Play.
